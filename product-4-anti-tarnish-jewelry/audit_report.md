### Section 3: RTO (Return to Origin) Defense Mechanics
**Score: 9.6 / 10 (Weight: 19.2 / 20)**

* **Telecom Regex Validation:** HTML5 pattern `pattern="[6-9][0-9]{9}"` strictly enforces Indian telecom standards (starting digits 6, 7, 8, 9), preventing bogus test orders.
* **Pincode Verification:** Form enforces `pattern="[0-9]{6}"` with `maxlength="6"`.
* **Prepaid UPI Incentive:** Prominent callout offering a **Flat ₹50 Instant Discount** for UPI/PhonePe payments over standard Cash on Delivery (COD), actively shifting orders to prepaid to reduce return logistics costs.
* **Multi-Field Address Cleansing:** Form separates Full Name, Mobile, Pincode, House/Flat No., and City/State to prevent incomplete address submissions.
